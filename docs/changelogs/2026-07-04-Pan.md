## Analyze Regime Distribution for Each Year
Turns out the soil moisture-based regimes are vary a lot across years, and can be particularly imbalance in specific years (e.g. almost 50% dry in 2023), but there isn't clear trend overall (e.g. 2024 and 2025 got back to the balanced distribution).

The droughtness in 2023 is unseen before, and no wonder why it hurts the test performance. So there's a gap of generalization of handling unseen weather trend there (i.e. the models are overfit on the train distribution).
