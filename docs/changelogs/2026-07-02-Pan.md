## Improving Hard-gating?
- Used $\beta = 0.2$ (copied from v24) and not much performance gain, possibly oracle gating already provides most of the performance benefits.
- **Update**: Ran a parameter sweep for $\beta \in [0.0, 0.5]$ and added `sweep_beta.py` to `derived_8.1_pos-eval-1.1`.
    - Found that **$\beta = 0.4$** is optimal for `derived_8.1_pos` splits, raising Model 5 (Oracle overall features) R² to **0.8729** (highest gating score on this dataset).
    - Dry Specialist R² improved significantly from $-0.0097$ (baseline) to **+0.1021** (at $\beta = 0.4$).
    - Transition Specialist R² improved from $-0.1548$ (baseline) to **-0.0117** (at $\beta = 0.5$).
    - Hard gating specialists benefit significantly from stronger recency weighting, although combined R² gains are muted due to the high baseline oracle gating ceiling.
