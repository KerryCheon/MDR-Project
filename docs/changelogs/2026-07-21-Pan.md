### The poor performance issue is now clear
The root cause of the poor performance in `derived_8.3` is now clear: the mountain stations (`MartenRidge_WA_999` and `RainyPass_WA_711`) with snowpack and frozen soil issues are causing the overall $R^2$ to drop significantly since they behaves very differently from ground stations.

Now we should consider removing these stations (even though they has valid data) because the terrain are rather unusual, thus there're are many in-situ stations on these mountains.

On the other hand, these stations with usual patterns might be beneficial from LSTM hybrid models.
