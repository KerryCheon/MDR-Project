## Hyperparameter Sweep
### Fewer Iteration
Tried the SOTA combination from `derived_8.2-hyperparameters-1.3` with different number of steps, turns out 1500 steps is enough to achieve similar performance to 8000 steps, saving about 80% of computational cost.

So even with lower learning rate, we don't really need that many steps.

### Generalization in terms applying to other models
Replicated V25 notebook as `derived_8.0-optimization-1.0`, did got marginally better than the original to 0.8253 from 0.8224. This confirmed the new hyperparameters are generalizable.

However, there's a reproducibility shift that the re-run original model only got 0.8190 on my machine.
