## Compute estimate & risks

- 96 jobs (84 LOSO folds + 12 full-baseline); observed effective ~21-35 s GPU per fold
  (XGBoost serializes on the single H100 even with 8 workers) → **~45-70 min wall**, fits the
  2 h limit with margin. Wall kill → re-submit (per-fold resume, data_version 1).
- 8 workers on a 6-CPU allocation = mild oversubscription (eval-2.0 ran the same; user
  requested 8 workers explicitly).
- New configs avoid the eval-1.1 delta-additions chain entirely (no per-regime selection
  exists). Inherited caveat (audit doc 20260812-clustering-methodlogy-audit.md): the 54
  backbone itself was test-selected upstream — disclose alongside test metrics as in eval-1.3.
- The K=3/4 regimes fragment stations (gating analysis: mean station purity 0.833 / 0.695 on
  trainval) and are partially empty for some held-out folds — the evaluator's existing
  mean-fallback (predict fold trainval mean for empty clusters, mirroring eval-1.1) covers
  this.
