## 1. Analysis of `derived_8.4-eval-mlp-2.1` (what we learned; drives every 2.2 decision)

### 1.1 Headline results (from the executed 2.1 notebook / README)

- **2.1 is an honest negative against 2.0.** The 3-seed val winner
  `w512x512x512_d0.3_huber0.05_lr6e-4` (mixed) → test R² 0.7844, below 2.0's
  2-seed honest single (0.7903) and its val top-5 ensemble (0.8003); 2.1's
  mixed val top-3/5/10 ensembles (0.7839/0.7831/0.7839) and cross-family
  ensemble (0.7931) also fail to beat 0.8003 / 0.7932.
- **SWA is closed out, with proof.** RNG guard → 136/136 swa-live-vs-anchor
  val-curve pairs bit-identical; 0/152 (seed, specialist) deployments across
  starts {0.6, 0.7, 0.75, 0.8, 0.85}. **No GPU re-spend on SWA in 2.2.**
- **Val selection is the bottleneck (headline negative).** Spearman(val, test)
  at 3-seed aggregation: +0.504 (96), **−0.555 (54)**, **−0.309 (mixed)**.
  Worse: the 54-family 1-seed val winner (`w320x320_d0.3_gelu_lr6e-4`) was the
  test-best; seed-averaging *flipped* the winner to `w512x512x512_d0.3_huber0.1`
  (test 0.7713). More seeds on the same val split did not fix the noise.
- **Debias:** 54-family median bias²/MSE 0.8 % (criterion met); 96 (13.9 %)
  and mixed (12.7 %) still miss the <5 % criterion. 11/46 96-configs are
  already <5 % (mixup, d0.6, small nets at lr3e-4) — the pool median is the
  problem, not the existence of unbiased configs.

### 1.2 The evidence that shapes the 2.2 sweep (from `sweep_results.csv`)

1. **The 54-family lr6e-4 × gelu region is the strongest untapped lever.**
   gelu + mse + d0.3 + **lr6e-4** beats every other 54 config at every width
   tested: w320² 0.7935 (test-best of 2.1), w384² 0.7906, w448² 0.7893,
   w640² 0.7860 (vs lr3e-4: 0.7881/0.7865/0.7696/0.7790). Widths **below 320
   are untested at lr6e-4** ({192, 224, 256, 288}); the peak may sit at
   ~256–320. lr1e-4 is uniformly bad (0.67–0.75) → **dropped**.
2. **The mixed-family test-best is `w448x448x448_d0.3_huber0.1_gelu` (0.7940,
   val rank 34!)** — a *gelu × 3-layer* cell that only exists at lr3e-4 (grid D
   was act × depth at fixed lr). **gelu × lr {6e-4, 1e-3} is untested
   everywhere in mixed** (2-layer and 3-layer), and 3-layer consistently beats
   2-layer in mixed (512³ 0.7836 vs 512² ~0.77–0.78 at lr6e-4/1e-3).
3. **The mixed huber-δ × lr surface at 512³ is flat (~0.778–0.785)** with the
   val winner at huber0.05 × lr6e-4; δ {0.03, 0.08} and lr {4e-4, 8e-4} are
   untested refinements. mixup and wd1e-3 are neutral-to-negative at 512³
   (0.7676–0.7769 vs anchor 0.7761) → **no mixup/wd re-spend in mixed**.
4. **96-family small nets are under-trained at the 400-epoch cap** (best_epoch
   380–395 ≈ max_epochs). lr6e-4 (between 3e-4 and 1e-3) is untested for 96;
   lr1e-3 overshoots (0.71–0.74); huber at lr3e-4 is mixed (0.7774 best);
   mixup kills bias (w256x256_d0.5_mixup0.2: 0.009 %) but costs test (0.7720).
   Levers: lr {4e-4, 6e-4, 8e-4}, huber × lr, **max_epochs {500, 600} probes**,
   3-layer small nets, mixup × lr.
5. **96-family val selection keeps picking the big net** (`w512x512x512_d0.3_lr1e-3`
   → test 0.7595) while the test-best is `w256x256_d0.5` (0.7834, val rank 40/46).
   The new val-year diagnostic (2021 vs 2022, section 3.2) is the honest way to
   understand this without touching the selection rule.

## 8. Risks & mitigations

- **The 54/mixed val noise persists** — structural, per 2.1. The 2.2
  mitigation is the val-year diagnostic (does val-2021 or val-2022 predict
  test better? does the winner flip under val-year-drop?) plus dense seed
  coverage of the top configs; the selection rule itself is untouched
  (protocol). If the winner still flips, it is reported honestly.
- **96 debias misses <5 %** — the pool is small-net-dominated by construction
  (11/46 of 2.1's pool already <5 %); the max_epochs / lr6e-4 / huber / mixup
  levers are the honest attempt; reported honestly either way.
- **`gpu_debug` occupancy** — 2 H100s total, other users observed; the job may
  queue. The 2 h limit bounds the wait; if the partition stays full we ask the
  user before falling back to `--partition=gpu`.
- **Cross-node bit-identity not guaranteed** (PTX-JIT/driver/cuDNN) — the
  anchor stack check is evidence (2.1 reproduced 2.0 exactly on a different
  node); reported with the same caveat.
- **~508 jobs is a long, failure-prone run** — resumable per phase (`--resume`),
  per-job logs, `seed_complete`/`seed_resumable` invalidate-and-retry; a single
  worker crash only costs that job.
- **1.25 h wall target missed** — phase counts and top-Ns are knobs; trims keep
  the session inside the 2 h cap; if faster, extra phase-2/3 seeds absorb the
  spare allocation.
