# Feature-selection 2.0 derived 8.3 evidence and handoff

## Interpretation boundary

The `derived_8.3-feature-selection-2.0` pipeline completed all 13 stages on CUDA with four workers. This document interprets the completed `final`, `nested`, `nested_locked_outer`, `crossed_candidates_locked_outer`, and `progressive_crossed_locked_outer` artifacts.

The method is an unchanged rerun of feature-selection 2.2 except for the documented derived 8.3 dataset and V0-router substitutions. All 2023–2025 results are retrospective because `derived_8.3-eval-1.0` already consumed that test period. They cannot support a new unbiased performance claim, and none of the observations below may be used to relabel that period as a fresh holdout.

Use [`README.md`](README.md) for the implementation, [`PROTOCOL.md`](PROTOCOL.md) for the interpretation contract, [`RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md) for canonical tables, and [`artifacts/report/`](artifacts/report/) for normalized evidence.

## Executive diagnosis

The rerun is operationally successful and methodologically faithful, but it does not justify replacing the derived 8.3 V0 baseline. The selected global arms look stronger during the original development re-score and then all fall below V0 in the retrospective period. That reversal is the central result: the development score is not reliable evidence of transfer.

The nested correction preserves the intended leakage boundary, yet its selected list also fails to transfer. Candidate winners depend strongly on the evaluation protocol, no full-training aggregate winner matches the saved selector winner, and the derived 8.3 nested candidate ordering is negatively associated with retrospective ordering. These observations implicate validation geometry and ranking stability rather than a single missing candidate count.

The V0-full router was fitted and frozen with the intended scope in each arm, but both regimes selected zero delta features. Frozen shared-only, frozen shared-plus-delta, and refit shared-plus-delta predictions are consequently equivalent. The MoE result is therefore evidence against additional expert complexity in this configuration, not evidence that a different router or expert learner would fail.

## What worked and should be retained

- The exact ordered 50-feature V0 list is loaded from versioned derived 8.3 metadata and recorded with its source hash.
- The original arm fits routing on its development pool; the nested arm fits preprocessing and K-means on inner training data and persists the frozen router.
- Station and date fields define folds without entering the predictor matrix.
- Nested candidate generation does not read outer-period labels.
- Locked-outer reproduction is exactly equivalent to the nested saved result.
- Split hashes, fold membership, router provenance, atomic completion markers, and a resumable 13-stage journal make the result auditable.
- The retained derived 8.0 control shows that the transfer problem is not unique to the new dataset substitution.

These are methodological and retrospective findings, not a new SOTA claim.

## Evidence-led interpretation

### Development selection does not transfer

The original derived 8.3 selected arm beats V0 only in the non-independent development re-score. All original, nested, and crossed selected global arms lose to V0 in the retrospective test report. V0 should remain the deployment reference until a development-only procedure demonstrates stable improvement and is then confirmed on untouched data.

The retained derived 8.0 control also remains below its hand-authored baseline. This makes a dataset-only explanation inadequate: the common selector/evaluation geometry is itself part of the observed failure.

### Candidate ranking is protocol-sensitive

Held-station selection, row-pooled scoring, and full-training aggregate scoring do not identify the same candidate. Full-training winners never match the saved selector winners in the alignment summary. For the current nested arm, candidate ranks transfer particularly poorly to the retrospective period. Feature-list overlap is also low across the original, nested, crossed, and V0 lists.

The next experiment should improve repeated development-only validation and ranking stability before expanding the candidate path. Retrospective ceilings are diagnostic only and must not become tuning targets.

### Shift is temporal and station-specific

The report shows lower target means in parts of the retrospective period, year-dependent bias, and severe errors concentrated at particular stations. Current-data outer-fold coverage is complete, so the failure cannot be dismissed as an unobserved assigned-station artifact. Pooled scores should be accompanied by station-macro risk, worst-station risk, year bias, and explicit station-year coverage.

### Temporal weighting is an arm choice

The beta variants do not move every artifact set in the same direction. Beta must remain a separately selected training-policy arm, using only development evidence; it cannot be treated as another correlated fold observation or chosen from the retrospective period.

### The configured MoE adds no selected specialization

Both regimes selected the shared feature set with no deltas, so the three MoE variants collapse to the same model inputs and predictions. Their small difference from the global arm comes from hard routing and separate fits, not selected regime-specific features. The experts also use only a minority of the router inputs as predictors, so any future router study needs a global-plus-router-input control before attributing effects to specialization.

## Next-experiment priorities

1. Reclassify 2017–2025 as development-only and reserve a future period and/or newly deployed ECE stations for the next unbiased evaluation.
2. Persist row-level out-of-fold predictions with station, date, year, fold family, feature-list hash, beta, and seed.
3. Evaluate repeated deterministic station partitions and multiple rolling outer origins using station-macro RMSE as primary risk, with pooled metrics, bias, and worst-station risk as diagnostics.
4. Stabilize the global ranking method across partitions and seeds before selecting feature count or beta; compare direct and progressive elimination without consulting retrospective ceilings.
5. Keep V0 as the reference model. Reconsider experts only after a global selected model improves robust development risk, and require experts to beat a global-plus-router-input control.
6. Freeze the complete selection, weighting, calibration, routing, and reporting contract before one-shot access to the next untouched holdout.
